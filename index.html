• FAQ

Questions, dumped & answered.

Is Brain Dump really for everyone? I’m not a “visual thinker”. +

Yes. Most users start with simple lists — groceries, birthdays, article links. The canvas only appears when you want it. We auto-color and link in the background, so you benefit without having to “mind map”.

How is this different from mymind / Notion / Obsidian?+

mymind archives beautifully, Notion organizes databases, Obsidian links notes — but none give you an infinite visual canvas *plus* auto-organization. Brain Dump is the desk, not the filing cabinet.

Is my data private? Do you train AI on it?+

Private by design. On-device indexing, end-to-end encryption, EU hosting (Frankfurt). We never train models on your dumps. Your thoughts stay yours — period.

What can I dump? Any format?+

Text, links, images, PDFs, voice notes, bookmarks — even handwritten photos (OCR). One global shortcut ⌘J. We detect type, extract text, and color-code: sage for projects, slate blue for ideas, parchment for inspiration.

Do I need to organize anything manually?+

No. That’s the point: *Remember everything. Organize nothing.* Drag to connect if you want, but auto-links and color already cluster related dumps. Manual is optional, magic is automatic.
